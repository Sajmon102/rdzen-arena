Know your tank.

Destroy shapes and enemy tanks to earn XP. Touching a shape damages both your tank and the shape. Every level grants one stat point. Click a numbered stat segment to buy up to that level, or MAX to spend available points on one stat. Upgrades are permanent. Eggs are white circles with 0.2 HP, worth 5 XP. Squares have 1 HP / 20 XP; triangles 10 HP / 100 XP; pentagons 75 HP / 500 XP; hexagons 200 HP / 2500 XP. All five families have normal, Beta, Alpha and Omega variants. Larger variants have more HP and award more XP. Explore the purple central nest for better XP and watch out for pink Crashers. Rare green Shiny shapes grant bonus XP. At levels 15 and 30 you can choose a new class or stay with the current one.
